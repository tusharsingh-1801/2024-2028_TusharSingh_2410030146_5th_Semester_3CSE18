## 📌 About the Internship

The internship focused on applying Python-based data analysis to a real-world restaurant dataset to convert raw records into evidence-based insights. The role involved collecting, cleaning, and interpreting data, visualizing information, and deriving meaningful insights. 

**Organization:** Cognifyz Technologies (ISO 9001:2015 and MSME certified), based in Nagpur, Maharashtra.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Inspect the restaurant dataset containing 9,551 records, verify data types, and treat missing values.
- Study the distribution and class imbalance of the Aggregate Rating target variable.
- Calculate descriptive statistics and identify leading cuisines, cities, and cost patterns.
- Visualize restaurant locations and measure the relationship between geographic coordinates and ratings.
- Compare the availability of table booking and online delivery and measure their relationship with ratings.
- Analyze restaurant counts and average ratings across four different price ranges.
- Engineer six numerical features from text and binary service fields to create a machine-readable dataset.

---

## 🗓️ Task-wise Curriculum (Level 1 & Level 2)

| Level & Task | Module | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 - Task 1 | Explore & Clean | Loaded the CSV, checked 9,551 x 21 shape, inspected types/missing values, filled nine missing cuisine entries, and analyzed rating imbalance. |
| Level 1 - Task 2 | Descriptive Analysis | Calculated numerical summaries and identified top cuisines, cities, cost patterns, and vote distributions. |
| Level 1 - Task 3 | Geospatial Analysis | Removed zero coordinates, mapped valid locations (India-region), and measured coordinate-rating correlations. |
| Level 2 - Task 1 | Compare Services | Calculated availability percentages, compared average ratings by booking status, and measured online delivery within price ranges. |
| Level 2 - Task 2 | Compare Prices | Found the most common price range, restaurant counts, average ratings, and the rating color associated with the highest-rated range. |
| Level 2 - Task 3 | Feature Engineering | Created six numerical features, measured rating correlations, and exported a 27-column enriched dataset. |
